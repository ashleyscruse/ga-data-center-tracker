# Georgia Data Center Tracker

> Mapping where data centers are landing in Georgia, and how communities are responding.

Georgia is in the middle of a data center boom. New facilities are being proposed, permitted, and built across the state at a pace that's reshaping local energy demand, land use, and community life. But the picture is fragmented. Information lives in scattered permit databases, utility filings, news coverage, and zoning board minutes, making it hard to see the full landscape or compare what's happening from one county to the next.

This project pulls that picture together.

## What This Project Tracks

| Dimension | What We're Capturing |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Proposals** | Where new data centers are being announced or planned |
| **Construction** | Where facilities are actively being built |
| **Operations** | Where data centers are already running |
| **Community Response** | How residents, advocacy groups, and local officials are reacting |

The result is a county-level view spanning all 159 Georgia counties, designed to feed into the [Drawdown Georgia Solutions Tracker](https://drawdownga.gatech.edu/) and support comparative analysis across the state.

## Why It Matters

Data centers are critical infrastructure for the digital economy, but their footprint is significant.

| Impact Area | Why It's a Concern |
|-------------|--------------------|
| **Energy** | Data centers draw enormous amounts of electricity, reshaping state and local energy planning |
| **Water** | Cooling systems can place heavy demand on local water resources |
| **Land Use** | Large facilities transform rural and exurban communities |
| **Community Voice** | Facilities often arrive in places that had little visibility into what was coming |

A county-level view of where data centers are concentrating, and how communities are reacting, is a starting point for more informed decisions by researchers, policymakers, advocates, and residents.

## Who This Is For

- **Researchers** studying the geography and impact of digital infrastructure
- **Policymakers** weighing energy, land use, and economic development trade-offs
- **Advocates and communities** tracking what's coming to their county
- **Journalists** reporting on data center growth in Georgia
